Built for real shop floors

No new app for your customers. No new habits for you. Just your WhatsApp, answering with discipline.

Reads photos and screenshots

Customers often send a picture instead of a name. The agent matches what it sees against your catalog and replies with the exact item, size and price.

Quotes only real stock

Every reply is grounded in your SKU list — size, color, price, stock. It never invents a product or a delivery promise.

One-click order desk

New orders land as pending. Accept or cancel with one click and the customer is notified on WhatsApp automatically.

Human handover

Custom or unclear requests are flagged for you instead of guessed at. Resolve the flag when you have answered.

Speaks your customer's language

Bangla, English or a mix of both — the agent mirrors the customer and keeps replies short and polite.

How it works

From signup to first answered message in an afternoon.

  1. 01

    Connect WhatsApp

    Scan one QR code from your dashboard. Your number stays yours — Nexora works through linked devices.

  2. 02

    Add your products

    Enter products with sizes, colors, prices and stock, or upload a photo for each item. The agent reads from this list only.

  3. 03

    Approve orders

    Customers chat as usual. You review pending orders, accept or cancel, and ship hand-offs are recorded per order.
